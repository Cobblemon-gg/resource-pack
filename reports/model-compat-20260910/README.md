# Model compatibility fixes — 2026-09-10

Confirmed client log errors: missing fallback posers for Latias, Latios and Beedrill. Mega-only pack files occupied the official 1.8 base resolver paths. Restored normal/shiny/alpha definitions and retained Mega definitions at base priority so skins can override them correctly.

Pidgeot: Journey Mounts 1.7.2 supplies old geometry without neck2/head_ai, while the official 1.8 poser expects those bones. The overlay now supplies the official 1.8 geometry at the conflicting path. Movie and Greek skins retain their old models and use isolated legacy posers/animations. Pidgeotto Movie skins receive their own legacy animation namespace, compatible limb/wing poses and original portrait framing.

Related verified legacy skin/poser pairings were repaired for Altaria (Angel), Crobat (Halloween/Karma), Houndoom (Halloween/Anniversary), Spoink and Zen Darmanitan (including Anniversary). Mega Altaria/Houndoom skins use their real head bones, and Mega variants explicitly select a Mega-compatible poser. Isolated legacy assets derive from Cobblemon commit 7e0c490c37; Pidgeotto framing/rig follows its old Kotlin model. Existing skin geometry/textures and Mega animations are retained. Imported Cobblemon assets remain subject to MPL-2.0.

Validation: inspected installed public Cobblemon 1.8 and Journey Mounts jars plus the frozen client baseline; scanned 1,026 species; checked complete base fallback collisions; verified selected roots/look/transformed parts, isolated animation references and packaged bytes. The broad audit is heuristic: conditional resolver selection, compiled posers, duplicate resource IDs and optional look bones require in-game review. Remaining audit warnings are NOT a verified list of crashes and were not blindly rewritten. None of the reported five species retains a detected bone mismatch in the post-fix audit. Latias/Latios separately passed 216 effective normal/shiny/alpha/skin checks.

Both ZIPs rebuilt. Use dist/resource-pack.zip for baseline clients or dist/resource-pack-full.zip for the full fallback.
